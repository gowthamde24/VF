Figure 2: Architectural Pivot

IP54 Environmental Isolation & BCM2711 (Pi 4) Thermal Mitigation Strategy

External Environment: Extreme Biological Humidity & Nutrient Splashing

Strategic Countermeasures Executed

STRATEGY I

Thermal Downgrade

  • ▪ SoC Replacement: Transitioned from Pi 5 to Raspberry Pi 4 Model B (BCM2711).
  • ▪ Clock Speed Reduction: Base clock reduced from 2.4 GHz to 1.5 GHz.
  • ▪ Net Effect: Substantially lower localized thermal output, eliminating the sharp temperature differential causing micro-condensation.
STRATEGY II

IP54 Sealing

  • ▪ Topology Change: Exposed breadboard topology entirely abandoned.
  • ▪ Ingress Protection (IP): Encased in industrial box rated IEC 60529 IP54.
  • ▪ 5 = Robust Dust Protection
    4 = Multi-directional Splash Guard
STRATEGY III

Physical Isolation

  • ▪ Custom Engineering: 3D-modeled standoffs designed to elevate computational logic.
  • ▪ Mechanical Shielding: Physically isolates sensitive PCB contacts from accidental pooling or bottom-up condensation.
  • Result: Zero Subsequent SBC Failures
